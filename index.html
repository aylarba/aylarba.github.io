01 — About

Background

I'm a Bioinformatics Scientist working within a pediatric cancer research core, where I develop and run NGS analysis pipelines — RNA-seq, WGS/WES, and DNA methylation — across large harmonized cohorts, alongside HLA typing and neoantigen prediction work. I build and maintain CWL workflows, run compute on AWS and CAVATICA/Seven Bridges, and work across HPC environments in Python, R, and Bash.

Before this, my graduate research focused on LC/MS metabolomics — quantifying small molecules to understand disease-relevant biology, which is where my interest in translating raw signal into biological insight first took shape. I hold an M.S. in Bioinformatics and a B.S. in Chemistry.
